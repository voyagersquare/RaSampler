RaSampler - Independent Audio
Windows 10/11, 64-bit

Close your DAW and any running RaSampler windows before continuing.

The VST3 instrument is installed for all users in the standard location:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\RaSampler.vst3

Choose "VST3 instrument only" if you only use RaSampler inside a DAW.
The optional standalone application is installed in:
C:\Program Files\Independent Audio\RaSampler

After installation, rescan VST3 instruments in your DAW. RaSampler appears
under the manufacturer Independent Audio. Its plugin identity is preserved
so existing RaSampler projects continue to load it.

If an older version was manually copied to another VST3 scan folder, remove
that old RaSampler.vst3 copy to avoid duplicate entries. Keep only the new
installed copy. Do not remove your samples, presets or DAW projects.

To remove this installation, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps >
RaSampler > Uninstall. Your own samples, presets and projects are preserved.

Documentation and third-party licence notices are included with the installation.
